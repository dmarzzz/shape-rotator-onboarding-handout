§ 01 Your First Week — 5 steps · ~30 min · everything below opens a GitHub PR

Welcome. These are the first five things to do once you're at the Convent and the field-guide app is running. Each one closes a small loop between you and the cohort. Start with step 01 so the rest of us can find you.

§ 03 The Default Week
Morningsevery weekday Heads-down. No programming until lunch. Mornings are protected.
5 – 7 PMmon · tue · thu Cohort sessions. Workshops, talks, whiteboarding. ~3–4 per week.
Wednesdayall day No required meetings. Protected focus day.
9 – 10 PMrotating Salon · roast · improv night. Opt-in, ~1 night a week.
Weekendsat + sun Off by default. Anything programmatic is opt-in.
§ 04 Success Vectors
i. Productization
Is this a product people want? PMF indicators, organic trajectory, distribution-to-market readiness.
ii. Research lineage
IC3 / Flashbots IP picked up and pushed forward. Nice-to-have, not required.
iii. Collaborative contribution
How much have you helped other projects? Retroactively attributed at graduation via peer-rubric review.

You'll be peer-reviewed on all three at graduation. Each cluster (commercial-viability / growth / commons) is judged on a different mix.
